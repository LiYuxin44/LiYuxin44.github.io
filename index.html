Yuxin Li

PhD candidate, Nanyang Technological University. Speech and multimodal agents.

Yuxin Li is a PhD candidate at Nanyang Technological University in Singapore, advised by Prof. Cuntai Guan and Prof. Eng Siong Chng. Yuxin builds speech and multimodal agents that understand, reason and act through natural spoken interaction, with a focus on reinforcement learning for audio LLMs, full-duplex spoken dialogue and agentic tool use. Yuxin comes from a speech background, spanning ASR, TTS, speech enhancement, speech emotion recognition and full-duplex interaction.

Yuxin is currently an Audio LLM research intern at Anuttacon, building multimodal post-training for audio understanding, reasoning and agentic. Before that, at StepFun, Yuxin was a core author of Step-Audio 2, Step-Audio-R1, Step-Audio 2.5 and DuplexSLA. Yuxin's clinical work at NTU studies speech-based depression assessment and how to keep speech models from leaning on the words instead of the voice.

Yuxin is open to collaboration and to research roles on speech and multimodal agents. Email is the best way to reach out.
